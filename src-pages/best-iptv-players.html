Methodology

How this ranking is built

Our /10 scores are editorial judgements — opinions formed from features officially documented by each developer plus real-world use, not instrumented lab tests. Five factors are weighted as follows:

  • Interface & usability (30%) — remote navigation, clarity, learning curve.
  • EPG capability (25%) — speed, supported formats, channel matching.
  • Platform coverage (20%) — device reach and quality on each platform.
  • Playback reliability (15%) — buffer management, codec support.
  • Value (10%) — what's free, what's paid, and whether it's worth it.

We accept no payment, commission or sponsorship from any app developer. Rankings are revisited whenever an app changes materially — including store removals, which we note in reviews.

FAQ

Questions about this ranking

What is the best IPTV player for Android TV?
In our view, TiviMate — the cable-style guide and class-leading EPG make it the benchmark. OTT Navigator is the better pick if you want deep customisation.
What is the best player for Samsung and LG Smart TVs?
IBO Player Pro is our top pick for Tizen and webOS. Smart IPTV (SIPTV) and Net IPTV are solid alternatives with the same web-portal model.
Are these apps legal to use?
Yes — all are legitimate software in official stores. Legality is determined by your content source: use licensed services only. Our legality & safety guide has the full picture.
Are these scores from lab testing?
