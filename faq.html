FAQ

Honest answers to the biggest IPTV questions

Collected from real reader questions — answered with nothing to sell you.

Basics & legality

Is IPTV legal?
The technology is legal — telecom TV services worldwide are licensed IPTV, and player apps like TiviMate or VLC are legitimate software. What's illegal is a service selling channel access without broadcast rights. Enforcement has intensified across the US, UK and EU — including, in some cases, consequences for users. Read the full guide.
What's the difference between IPTV and Netflix or Pluto TV?
All are streaming over the internet. Netflix and Pluto TV are complete licensed services (app + content). \"IPTV\" in everyday conversation usually means a player app plus a linear-channel source you add yourself. The player is neutral; the source's licensing determines legality.
Are \"fully loaded\" streaming boxes safe to buy?
No. Pre-configured boxes promising every channel combine unlicensed services with documented malware risk — research has found nearly half of tested illicit streaming apps contain malicious code. Buy hardware empty from known brands; install your own apps from official stores. More in our device guide.
Can viewers — not just sellers — get in trouble?
Enforcement concentrates on operators and sellers, but that's no longer the whole story: a 2025 UK case included a sentence for watching illegal streams, and over a thousand UK users received formal warning letters. It was never a sound risk to take; it's now a demonstrably real one. See our news report.

Apps & devices

What's the best player app for my device?
Short version: TiviMate for Android TV/Google TV/Fire TV; IBO Player Pro for Samsung/LG; GSE Smart IPTV for iPhone/iPad; IPTV Smarters for one app everywhere. Full reasoning on the Best Players page.
Why can't my Samsung/LG TV install TiviMate?
TiviMate is Android-only. Samsung runs Tizen and LG runs webOS — different systems with their own stores. Use a Tizen/webOS player, or add an Android TV device to one of your TV's HDMI ports.
What are M3U, Xtream and EPG?
M3U = a playlist file of channel URLs. Xtream Codes = an API login that delivers channels, VOD and guide data together. EPG = programme schedule data (XMLTV format). Full explainer with comparison table.
Are free app versions enough, or should I pay?
For basic use, most free tiers suffice. Paying typically unlocks multiple playlists, recording and customisation — worth it for daily viewers. Note: an app fee is a software licence, not a content subscription; two different things.
Why did an app disappear from my app store?
Player apps are periodically removed after rights-holder complaints or court orders (IPTV Smarters Pro's removal from Google Play after a LaLiga court order is the best-known case) — and sometimes re-listed later. Our reviews note availability caveats per app, and it's why we never assume an app is permanently available on any store.

Technical problems

Why does my stream keep buffering?
Nine common causes, from weak Wi-Fi to undersized buffers, are worked through in our buffering guide. First steps: speed-test on the TV device itself, and test the problem stream in VLC on a computer.
Why is my TV guide empty or showing wrong times?
An empty guide is usually a tvg-id matching problem; wrong times are usually a timezone setting. Both are fixed step-by-step in the EPG guide.
How fast does my internet need to be?
Practical figures: a stable 5–8 Mbps for HD, 25 Mbps+ for 4K — per simultaneous stream in the household. Stability matters more than headline speed; ethernet or close-range 5 GHz Wi-Fi makes the biggest difference.
