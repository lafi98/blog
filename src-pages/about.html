About Us

A small lab for a noisy corner of streaming

SignalLab exists because of a simple observation: most information about IPTV is written by people trying to sell you something.

What we do

We review IPTV player software, write setup and troubleshooting guides, explain streaming technology in plain language, and report industry news that matters to viewers — from platform shifts like Fire TV's move to Vega OS to the enforcement actions reshaping the market.

Our coverage is written for a global English-speaking audience and organised by device and topic rather than by country — because a TiviMate setup works the same in London, Toronto and Sydney.

What we DON'T do

  • We don't sell IPTV subscriptions — and never will.
  • We don't supply playlists, channels or content of any kind.
  • We don't take commissions from subscription sellers or app developers.
  • We don't recommend unlicensed providers, however often we're asked.

📋 Editorial policy

Every review states strengths and weaknesses. Every news report names its sources. Claims we can't verify ourselves are labelled as opinion or attributed. Errors get corrected openly when reported — corrections with sources attached get priority.

⚖️ Legal position

We endorse only the use of properly licensed services. Our guides emphasise legitimate sources, and pages like the legality & safety guide and the reseller explainer exist specifically to help readers recognise and avoid illegal operations.

🔬 Methodology

Review scores are editorial judgements across five openly weighted factors — not lab tests, and we don't pretend otherwise. Store availability changes are tracked and noted per app. Full details on the methodology page.
